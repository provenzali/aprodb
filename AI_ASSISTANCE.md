# AI-assisted development disclosure

AProDB was conceived and is directed by **Andrea Provenzali**, who wrote and
approved the project specification, selected the architecture and licensing,
and retains editorial responsibility for the repository.

OpenAI Codex has been used as a development assistant for implementation,
tests, documentation, review and repository preparation. Its suggestions are
not accepted automatically: they are selected, revised, integrated and tested
under human direction. The AI service is not listed as an author, copyright
holder or project contributor.

This disclosure is voluntary provenance information. It does not alter the
AGPL-3.0-only and Apache-2.0 licensing boundary described in
[LICENSING.md](LICENSING.md), and it is not a claim that AProDB is an AI system.

At the assessment date below, AProDB contains no bundled AI model and makes no
runtime call to a generative-AI service. Its exact vector scoring and optional
GPU acceleration are deterministic database-compute functions. If a future
release introduces model inference, generated content or direct human/AI
interaction, the regulatory classification and this disclosure must be
reviewed again.

Current EU AI Act assessment: [docs/eu-ai-act-assessment.md](docs/eu-ai-act-assessment.md).
